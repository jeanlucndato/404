Vulnerability Disclosure & Ethical Hacking Portfolio

–

(Offensive Mindset)

              This capability profile covers bug bounty research within a program’s published scope: mapping an allowed attack surface, validating suspected weaknesses, assessing impact, and preparing a clear, responsible disclosure. No client engagement or specific vulnerability outcome is represented on this page.            
           
             
               
                 
                   
                     
                      Scope                     Program-approved assets                    
                 
                 
                   
                     
                      Research focus                       Web and API security                    
                 
                 
                   
                     
                      Validation                       Impact assessed against program rules                  
                 
                 
                   
                     
                      Disclosure                       Responsible and policy-aligned          
                 
               
             
           
         
             
               

                                      (Advanced Analysis)                                  

             
             
               
Begin with the program policy and asset list. Map only the permitted attack surface, identify likely entry points, and prioritize checks that fit the published scope and testing limits.
             
             
               
Validate suspected issues with the least intrusive proof needed to explain impact. Avoid accessing or retaining sensitive data, follow program rules, and submit a concise report with reproduction steps and remediation context.
