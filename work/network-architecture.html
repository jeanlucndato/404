Network Architecture

–

(Intro)

              This capability overview covers secure network architecture, routing, segmentation, and resilience. Work is planned around the environment, business requirements, and agreed scope. The page describes areas of practice and does not claim a named-client engagement or service-level result.
Scope Authorized environments and defined requirements
Sector Networks, infrastructure, and cloud connectivity
Services SD-WAN Deployment, BGP/OSPF Routing, VXLAN/EVPN, Network Hardening
Approach Documented design, validation, and hardening

(Technical Scope)

Design work starts with business requirements, existing topology, and availability needs. Options can include segmentation, resilient routing, and documented failover behavior, validated against the agreed environment.
Implementation planning can cover routing, access controls, configuration management, and operational handover. Verification records what was reviewed and what follow-up actions remain for the system owner.
